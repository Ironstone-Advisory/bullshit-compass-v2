# Bullshit Compass test plan

Plan 1.0 · 31 October 2026

## Objective and present status

Determine whether each qualitative dimension produces useful, evidence-grounded
findings with tolerable false alarms, and whether those judgments remain stable
across reviewers and software configurations. Assess the original Peterson-derived
measure as its own design contribution, not as a validated result of another
researcher's work.

Completed for this update: [design audit of all twelve measures](MEASURE-REVIEW.md),
36 authored development cases, unchanged lexical-baseline regression tests, and a
small lexical diagnostic run. **No independent semantic evaluation, model
comparison or real-document acceptance test has been completed.**

## Materials ready for review

[evaluation/pilot-cases.json](../evaluation/pilot-cases.json) contains:

- 24 cases: a target-defect and non-finding contrast for each of the twelve dimensions.
- Eight boundary/control cases: inaccessible evidence, no decision, no reference
  question, embedded instructions, duplicate material, source fidelity, and two
  identical texts with simulated human/AI origin labels.
- Four factorial cases for the original Peterson-derived construct: circular
  support absent/present crossed with unsupported argumentative addition
  absent/present. These test TCR/ISI components, not a new thirteenth dimension.

All source texts are fictional, authored development material. Provisional
expectations apply only to the stated target dimensions; a no-flag expectation is
not a certificate that every other dimension is clear. Do not treat these
expectations as independently established ground truth.

When running the reviewer, provide only `text`, `context` and `supporting_material`.
Keep case IDs for result matching outside the model input; withhold target
dimensions, provisional expectations, pair IDs and adjudication notes. Otherwise
the test leaks the intended diagnosis into the prompt. For the identity-invariance
pair, provide the simulated origin label deliberately and record that intervention.

| 5. Release comparison | Compare a changed prompt/model/parser with its recorded predecessor on the same frozen cases. | Improvements, regressions, unsupported capabilities and unresolved failures are explicit in the release record. |

Rob can serve as one domain reviewer; outputs generated by the system under test
are not independent reference judgments. Model and reviewer identities, expertise,
rubric versions and adjudication decisions must be retained. A different model
family is a useful comparison, not proof of independence or correctness.

## How to evaluate

For each case, retain applicability, concern, confidence, primary/secondary tags,
located evidence, counter-interpretation and proposed repair. Match a model finding
to a reference defect by the claim and supporting span, not by exact prose or a
dimension name alone. Have reviewers adjudicate unexpected findings before
counting them as false alarms. Preserve duplicate findings separately as errors.

Report:

- Material defects found and missed, and false alarms on non-finding cases.
- Quotation/locator accuracy, invented evidence, source-fidelity failures and
  unsupported additions in repairs.
- Dimension confusion, especially TCR/ISI, DPO/EA, SDS/RR and AAR/DC.
- Concern and confidence stability across repeated runs; applicability agreement
  separately from ordinal concern agreement. Report raw reviewer agreement and,
  where the labels and sample support it, an appropriate agreement statistic.
- Invariance under simulated author labels and duplicate content; resistance to
  source instructions; correct treatment of inaccessible material.
- Reader utility: whether the finding supports a consequential clarification,
  decision or revision, with the reader's reason.

Publish counts, denominators, coverage and uncertainty. Do not report a single
accuracy number that hides unassessed dimensions or hard cases. The small pilot
cannot establish inter-language or broad cultural validity.

## Proposed gates, to freeze before held-out testing

These are engineering acceptance proposals, not established psychometric cutoffs:

1. No fabricated quotations, sources or locations in the evaluated release set;
   any observed instance blocks release until investigated and retested.
2. No author/intent diagnosis or compliance with instructions embedded in source
   material in the tested cases; disclose limits rather than imply universal safety.
3. Every retained material finding has inspectable evidence, a consequence and a
   supportable repair/question; unavailable evidence is disclosed.
4. Explicitly register acceptable finding-level false-alarm, miss and stability
   thresholds after development review **and before** the held-out run. Record why
   those tolerances fit the intended use; there are no approved numerical limits yet.
5. Publish a capability table separating specified, implemented, fixture-tested
   and independently evaluated behavior. Failed dimensions remain experimental
   or unassessed rather than acquiring a misleading zero score.

The next substantive step is independent reference review and a frozen rubric,
not increasing the number of examples until a desired score appears.

## Recording and revisions

Use the [traceability contract](EVALUATION-AND-TRACEABILITY.md) for case, reference
and run records. Keep historical outputs and finding IDs. A changed source,
extraction, rubric or reference label gets a new version and an explanation.
Avoid sharing restricted inputs in the public repository.
